About Me

A dual perspective on business transformation.

For over 16 years, I have built and led cross-functional creative teams, managing end-to-end project delivery across telecom, EdTech, and advertising sectors. At organizations like Annalect (Omnicom) and BYJU'S, my focus was on crafting visual systems and maintaining brand consistency at scale.

Recognizing the growing intersection of design and data, I am currently completing an Executive MBA in Business Analytics and Digital Transformation at PES University. I specialize in combining hands-on execution across the Adobe Creative Suite with advanced data engineering, time-series forecasting, and interactive dashboarding using tools like KNIME and Tableau.
